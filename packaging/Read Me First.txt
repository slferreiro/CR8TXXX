CR8TXXX: manage your Mixxx library and crates, and find damaged music files
(Apple silicon Macs, macOS 13 or later)

INSTALL
1. Drag CR8TXXX onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open CR8TXXX from Applications.

   The first time, macOS may say it "can't check CR8TXXX for malicious software"
   (it is signed by its developer but not yet notarized by Apple). Then:
     - click Done (or OK),
     - open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
       "CR8TXXX was blocked..." and click Open Anyway,
     - confirm with your password or Touch ID.
   This is needed only once.

FIRST RUN: "Set Up CR8TXXX"
A setup window checks what this Mac needs and asks before changing anything:
- Mixxx library: open Mixxx at least once first, with your music folder added in
  Mixxx > Preferences > Library. macOS protects Mixxx's library, so setup has a button
  that opens System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access: turn on CR8TXXX,
  then quit and reopen CR8TXXX.
- ffmpeg (used to check files other than MP3): if it isn't on this Mac, click
  Download & Install. It goes into CR8TXXX's own folder (no password needed, about 80 MB,
  needs the internet once). Without it, the damaged-file scan still checks MP3s.
Setup can be opened again any time: Help > Set Up CR8TXXX...

GOOD TO KNOW
- Changes are only made while Mixxx is closed; an orange banner says so while it's open.
- Before the first change of each session, CR8TXXX saves a backup copy of Mixxx's library
  (File > Show Backups in Finder).
- To remove CR8TXXX: drag it to the Trash, and delete the folder
  ~/Library/Application Support/CR8TXXX (backups, scan results, ffmpeg).
